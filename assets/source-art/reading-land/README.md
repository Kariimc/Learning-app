# The art Kariim actually wanted, and why the app is not using it

READ THIS BEFORE TOUCHING THE APP'S PICTURES.

**These 52 pictures and 37 voice clips are the assets Kariim wanted in Reading
Land.** They are not spares and they are not rejects. In his words, on
2026-08-22:

> "The reading land art is the actual assets for the app that I wanted. I had to
> settle for what is in the app currently because the model could not place them
> correctly/successfully."

So the art in `assets/images/` is a FALLBACK he settled for. It is not the
intended look. Nobody should tidy this folder away, and nobody should treat the
current pictures as the finished art direction.

## What blocked it

Placement. The pictures exist and the look is right; getting them into the right
places in the app is what failed. That is the job still to do, and it is the one
worth unblocking.

## What is here

52 pictures and 37 voice clips, generated 2026-06-24. Animals (owl, monkey,
bunny, dogs, cats), objects with faces (shapes, books, pencil, car, apple),
buttons, and storybook scenes.

They lived on Kariim's desktop in a folder called "Reading Land assets", outside
any project and with no backup anywhere, until 2026-08-22.

## Honest state of them

Gemini looked at all 52 pictures on 2026-08-22 and scored them 6 out of 10:

- the rendering is good and consistent: plush and clay textures, soft light,
  bright pastels
- **the words inside the pictures are garbled**, so any picture carrying text
  needs its words redone before it can ship
- heavy repetition: several goes at "The Cat Nap" and "The Sun and The Bird",
  and many near-identical buttons
- no exact duplicates

The voice clips have NOT been listened to. Nobody has checked them.

## Next step

Work out why placement failed before generating anything new. The assets are not
the problem.
